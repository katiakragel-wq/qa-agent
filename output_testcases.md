**Assumptions to confirm with the product owner:**
- Email matching is case-insensitive and surrounding whitespace is trimmed (TC-02, TC-03).
- Passwords are case-sensitive and not trimmed (TC-21, TC-22).
- Attempts made while the account is locked do not extend the lock (TC-09).
- Without "Remember me", the session ends when the browser closes (TC-26).
- The maximum password length is not stated in the ticket. TC-33 and TC-34 assume 128 characters.
